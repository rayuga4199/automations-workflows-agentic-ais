In service designs relying on a single coordinator, every back-stage process (scheduling, volunteer retention, data compliance, absence follow-up) funnels through one person. If that person leaves or is overwhelmed, the service collapses regardless of how well the front-stage artefacts are designed.

**Why:** Library digital inclusion programmes consistently under-resource coordination. The 2025 volunteer-attrition figure of 39% means coordinator churn is not hypothetical — it is likely.

**How to apply:** At framing and review stage, always ask: what breaks first if the coordinator is unavailable for two weeks? Design a named deputy role and a handover pack as non-negotiable deliverables, not optional add-ons. Flag this to the client as a resourcing decision before handoff.
